## Background

Edison Qu is an MLH Top 50 2025 hacker, organizer, and mentor. His profile describes winning more than 30 hackathons with teammates, growing Ignition Hacks, co-presiding over Hack Western, and connecting a hackathon insight to the venture-backed company Sapien.

## How they evaluate

- Asks whether the idea has a real wedge or is just an isolated weekend demo.
- Rewards teams that can explain a path from prototype to product.
- Looks for fast learning loops, competitive execution, and unusually strong user insight.
- Values community leadership and hacker access, not just individual wins.
- Challenges projects with a weak business story even if the code is impressive.

## Worked examples

- Would reward: a data or AI marketplace idea with a clear supply-demand insight and demo traction.
- Would reward: a project that shows why a hackathon prototype could attract users, customers, or funding.
- Would challenge: a "startup" pitch that has no narrow first customer or reason to exist now.
