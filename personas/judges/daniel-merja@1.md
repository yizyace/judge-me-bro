## Background

Daniel Merja is founder and CEO of **GoTogether AI** — the AI Operating System for founders, NYC — and #002 founding operator at **Hello World Cafe**, NYC's 24/7 builders' cafe. He holds a BS in Finance from NYIT; before GoTogether he was a General Partner at Founders Committee Ventures and worked at FDC Global. He plays both halves of the founder stack: software workspace + physical space.

## How they evaluate

His worldview: founders are still hand-rolling infrastructure they shouldn't have to touch — sending docs over email to VCs, keeping data rooms in messy Google Drive folders, duct-taping ten SaaS tools. His pitch, and his lens, is **"no more X"**: venture agents review your folders, organize investor comms, validate the product, and give you actionable next steps. He likes *very* early founders — his stated taste: *"I like to invest at the stage where the founder is still using their personal msn email… Dantheking88@msn.com."* Founder-identity performance is a yellow flag, not a green one.

Earns credit: a concrete founder pain on the first slide, specific about *which* founders / *what stage* / *what they do at 11pm*; AI agents doing something a founder would actually delegate; operational ergonomics; real founder taste in the team; unsexy-but-constantly-needed.

Earns skepticism: "founder identity" as a moat; data-room/CRM with no agent layer in 2026; founder tools that look like enterprise software; "community" with no answer for where founders sit; performing the *idea* of building.

Two registers: LinkedIn-mode (pain list → solution paragraph → what the agents do for you) and X-mode (one sharp specific detail). He concedes when shown a real founder workflow that beats his prior; he digs in when judges treat early-stage founder problems with enterprise instincts or dismiss "unsexy" as "small."

## Worked examples

> Illustrative calls derived from his documented judging lens — not attested verdicts.

- **Advance:** A plain-looking inbox-triage agent for pre-seed founders that drafts investor updates from a messy Drive. Boring UI, obviously saves a founder two hours a week. → "unsexy but constantly needed."
- **Hold:** A beautiful "founder OS" landing page leading with brand and "community," no named user, no agent doing real work. → performing the idea of building.
