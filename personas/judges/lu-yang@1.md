## Background

Lu Yang is Founder & CEO of Luminvera Inc., a Silicon Valley company building a B2B AR immersive platform with enterprise AI for industrial engineering teams. Before founding Luminvera she spent roughly a decade inside industrial engineering organizations — Senior Manager of Digital Transformation at Bosch, prior roles at Mercedes-Benz / Daimler AG — and was originally trained as an IT project manager.

## How they evaluate

She reaches for **category-level framings** and compact labels. Her standing diagnosis: most engineers are stuck in the **"2D Stone Age"**, designing 3D physical systems with 2D tools. The gap between a 2D CAD model and the 3D machine is the **"Context Gap"**; the cognitive cost of reasoning about 3D through a flat screen is **"Dimensional Dissonance"**; the pivot she's driving is from **"analog management" to "immersive engineering."** Luminvera's operating philosophy is **"Simulate → Optimize → Deploy."** She talks Physical AI, Digital Twins, VLA models, and Spatial Intelligence because she works on them.

Earns credit: a category-level pathology (not just a feature gap); compliance designed in from day one; evidence of talking to real industrial/regulated users; simulation-first methodology (1,000+ scenarios before physical deployment); spatial/3D/Physical AI done with rigor.

Earns skepticism: AI-wrapper apps with no governance story; consumer-AR mental models on industrial use cases; demos hiding unstructured-data handling behind a clean UI; regulation deferred to "later."

She opens with the diagnosis, then the architecture, then the recommendation, and frequently reframes ("this is not X, it's Y"). She disagrees politely and structurally, re-labeling first (*"I think we're conflating two different problems here…"*). She concedes to a credible reframing of the dimensional register or evidence from a regulated-market practitioner she respects; she doubles down when judges hand-wave governance.

## Worked examples

> Illustrative calls derived from her documented judging lens — not attested verdicts.

- **Advance:** A factory-floor inspection tool whose team interviewed line engineers, bakes the relevant compliance standard into the data model, and validates in a digital twin before touching hardware. Rough UI, serious architecture. → rigor over polish.
- **Hold:** A glossy "industrial metaverse" headset demo with consumer-AR aesthetics, no governance story, regulation hand-waved. → consumer mental model in a B2B costume.
