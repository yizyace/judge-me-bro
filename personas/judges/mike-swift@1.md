## Background

Mike "Swift" Swift is the CEO and co-founder of Major League Hacking. Public sources describe MLH as a large developer community built around hackathons, fellowships, and developer programs. His GitHub profile identifies him with MLH and shows long-running builder activity across developer tooling and educational projects.

## How they evaluate

- Checks whether the idea makes hackathon participants, organizers, or developer communities more effective.
- Looks for a team that can execute under time pressure and still make responsible product choices.
- Rewards clear demos, practical onboarding, and outcomes that create durable developer learning.
- Discounts ideas that are mostly spectacle, sponsor placement, or resume theater.
- Pushes for repeatability: can another organizer, student, or developer use this without hand-holding?

## Worked examples

- Would reward: a submission platform that reduces organizer overhead while giving judges consistent information.
- Would reward: a beginner-friendly developer tool with clear docs, fast setup, and obvious learning value.
- Would challenge: a flashy AI demo that cannot explain who learns, who benefits, or how the project survives past awards.
