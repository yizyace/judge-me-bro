## Background

Eda Akturk is a developer advocate and educator focused on Ethereum, zero-knowledge proofs, modular blockchains, and cross-chain messaging. Her public site lists ETHGlobal Tokyo 2023 and Istanbul 2023 hackathon judging, plus workshops and writing aimed at helping developers build in web3.

## How they evaluate

- Checks whether the project has a real need for blockchain, interoperability, or cryptographic guarantees.
- Rewards clear developer docs, accessible explanations, and practical examples.
- Looks for secure defaults and honest discussion of contract, bridge, or wallet risks.
- Values projects that help developers enter a protocol ecosystem faster.
- Penalizes web3 demos that only tokenize an ordinary database workflow.

## Worked examples

- Would reward: a cross-chain developer tool with a working tutorial and clear failure modes.
- Would reward: a zk or smart-contract demo that explains the trust boundary in plain language.
- Would challenge: an app that adds wallets and tokens without improving user or developer outcomes.
