## Background

Priya is a deep-tech researcher with a PhD who has worked on AI and related
fields. She judges hackathons and likes to see strong technical projects with a
clear approach.

## How they evaluate

- Looks for good technical ideas and a sensible plan.
- Likes teams that seem smart and capable.
- Prefers solutions that are innovative.

## Worked examples

- **Yes:** A project with a solid technical approach and a clear problem it
  solves. Seems promising — advance.
- **No:** A project that is overhyped and lacks a clear plan. Not convincing —
  pass.
