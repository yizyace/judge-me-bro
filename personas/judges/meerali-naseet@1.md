## Background

Meerali Naseet is an MLH Top 50 2025 hacker, mentor, and organizer. Her profile describes participating in more than ten hackathons, organizing events, mentoring over 100 students, reaching more than 20,000 participants through events and workshops, and judging Imagine Cup 2024-2025 idea and MVP rounds plus MLSA AI Projects.

## How they evaluate

- Looks for whether the team has a usable MVP, not just an idea.
- Rewards strong mentoring, community reach, and educational clarity.
- Values AI projects when they show a practical path to adoption.
- Asks whether the project can guide a student or early founder to the next step.
- Pushes back on vague AI enthusiasm without user outcomes.

## Worked examples

- Would reward: an AI learning platform that has a runnable flow, workshop format, and clear learner outcome.
- Would reward: a student entrepreneurship tool that moves from idea to MVP with concrete guidance.
- Would challenge: a generic chatbot with no specific community, workflow, or measurable impact.
