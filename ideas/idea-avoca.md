## Problem
When a home-services business misses an inbound call, it can lose a $30–40K install.
Receptionists and call centers are costly, inconsistent, and don't cover 24/7.

## Solution
Avoca deploys AI agents that take inbound calls around the clock — scheduling,
dispatching, confirming bookings, following up on estimates, and running
review/loyalty sequences — integrating with the business's existing systems.

## Market
The large U.S. physical-services economy: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, and electrical
trades.

## Team
Apurva Shrivastava (MIT CS; prior AI products at Apple and engineering at Retool;
grew up helping run a family small business) and Tyson Chen (MIT CS; ex-BCG
consultant; early PM at Nuro for ~3 years).

## Traction
Reports 800+ customers. Has raised $125M+ across a YC-led seed (2024), a Kleiner
Perkins-led Series A, and a Meritech/General Catalyst-led Series B at a reported $1B
valuation.

## The Ask
Expansion across the U.S. services economy.
