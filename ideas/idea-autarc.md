## Problem
Energy and HVAC installers lose many hours per project to manual work scattered
across disconnected tools.

## Solution
autarc unifies CRM, ERP, email, and planning — accessible via WhatsApp/iMessage — and
uses LiDAR for spatial analysis plus computer vision for automated property
assessment, reporting ~50% shorter project timelines and 24+ hours saved per home
energy project.

## Market
The heat-pump, solar, and battery installation industry, driven by energy-transition
and regulatory pressure, starting in Germany and Europe.

## Team
Etienne-Noel Krause (CEO; prior HVAC-industry experience), Thies Hansen (COO;
background in logistics/process optimization, startups, and VC), and Marius Seufzer
(CTO; formerly Lead iOS Engineer at Sameday Health, built Passkeys for Apple).

## Traction
Berlin-based team reports ~30 employees, 380 customers, and seven-figure annual
revenue.

## The Ask
Scaling the installer base.
