## Problem
Engineering teams scatter API keys, DB credentials, and certs across `.env` files,
Slack, and config systems — "secret sprawl" — creating security risk and operational
drag.

## Solution
Infisical is an open-source, end-to-end encrypted platform that centralizes secrets,
certificate management, and privileged access, with self-hosting for teams that need
to keep data in their own infrastructure.

## Market
Secrets, identity, and access management for engineering orgs, challenging incumbents
like HashiCorp Vault.

## Ask
Raised a $16M Series A (June 2025, led by Elad Gil; ~$19M+ total) to expand from
secrets into a unified secrets + PKI + access-management platform for enterprises.
Reported cash-flow positive, 20x YoY revenue growth, 40M+ downloads.
