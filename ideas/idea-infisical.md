## Problem
Engineering teams scatter API keys, DB credentials, and certs across `.env` files,
Slack, and config systems — "secret sprawl" — creating security risk and operational
drag.

## Solution
Infisical is an open-source, end-to-end encrypted platform that centralizes secrets,
certificate management, and privileged access, with self-hosting for teams that need
to keep data in their own infrastructure.

## Market
Secrets, identity, and access management for engineering orgs, challenging
incumbents like HashiCorp Vault.

## Team
Vlad Matsiiako (CEO; prior experience at AWS), Maidul Islam, and Tony Dang (prior
experience at Figma and Bunq). The founders met at Cornell University.

## Traction
Reported cash-flow positive with 20x YoY revenue growth and 40M+ downloads. Raised a
$16M Series A (June 2025, led by Elad Gil; ~$19M+ total, with a 2023 seed backed by
Gradient/Google and YC).

## The Ask
Expanding from secrets into a unified secrets + PKI + access-management platform for
enterprises.
