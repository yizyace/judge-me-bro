## Problem
People who share pitch decks, contracts, and confidential documents have no
visibility into how those documents are viewed, and proprietary tools like DocSend
are closed and costly.

## Solution
Papermark is an open-source, self-hostable data room offering page-by-page view
analytics, dynamic watermarking, NDA gating, custom domains, and granular
permissions.

## Market
Document sharing and virtual data rooms for fundraising, M&A, due diligence, and
sales.

## Team
Marc Seitz (founder; GitHub handle `mfts`) — self-taught programmer with 10+ years in
software and a background in finance and physics. Founder count is disputed across
sources: GitHub and StarterStory credit Marc Seitz as sole founder, while Tracxn and
some secondary coverage describe a Marc Seitz + Iuliia Shnai duo.

## Traction
Bootstrapped with no outside funding; reported profitable. 8K+ GitHub stars. A
~$900K ARR figure appears in secondary reporting but is not confirmed by the company.

## The Ask
No raise; growth is driven by open-source distribution rather than venture capital.
