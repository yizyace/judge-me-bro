## Problem
First-of-a-kind grid fusion reactors will be expensive, high-maintenance, and run at
low capacity factor, pushing grid electricity cost 5–10x higher.

## Solution
Maritime Fusion builds an HTS tokamak designed for ships, which need ~15x less power
and tolerate lower up-time. Running at reduced power density eases the
materials/engineering challenges and lets the reactor match alternative-fuel costs
without emissions. Near-term, the team commercializes HTS cable technology for power
distribution.

## Market
Decarbonizing global commercial shipping, plus Department of Defense mobile-power
needs.

## Ask
Capital and partners to advance HTS tokamak physics toward commercial marine
reactors. The team brings 10+ years combined plasma-physics, nuclear, and electrical
experience from SpaceX, Tesla, and Princeton.
