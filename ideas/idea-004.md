## Problem

We believe pets are underrepresented in web3 and that owners lack a way to
immortalize their pet's emotional journey on an immutable ledger. Honestly, we
have not been able to point to anyone who has asked for this. We ran no customer
interviews; the idea came from thinking blockchain plus AI plus pets would be a
fun combination. There is no identifiable pain here — owners already share pet
photos for free on Instagram and TikTok and seem perfectly content. This is a
solution we built first and went looking for a problem afterward.

## Solution

AuraChain uses an AI model to analyze a daily pet photo and output a "vibe
score" (1–100), which we mint as an NFT on a custom L2 we are building. Owners
collect their pet's vibe NFTs, trade them on a marketplace, and earn $AURA
tokens for daily streaks. The architecture is heavy — a bespoke chain, a token,
a marketplace, and an ML pipeline — for an outcome (a cute number under a pet
photo) that needs none of it. The blockchain adds cost, latency, and confusion
without solving anything a normal app couldn't, and the "AI vibe score" is a
gimmick with no ground truth. Nothing here is differentiated or defensible; it's
a stack of buzzwords in search of a reason to exist.

## Market

We claim a TAM of "all pet owners globally," roughly a billion people, which is
the kind of top-down number that means nothing. There is no evidence any
meaningful slice of pet owners wants to buy, mint, or trade pet-mood NFTs, and
the crypto-collectibles market they'd be sold into has cratered. Realistic
reachable demand is, as far as we can tell, approximately zero. We have no
pricing, no business model beyond hand-waving at token speculation, and no path
to revenue we can articulate.

## Team

Solo founder, Blake, who is enthusiastic but is a first-time founder with no
background in machine learning, blockchain infrastructure, the pet industry, or
consumer social distribution — i.e. none of the four hard things this idea would
require. There is no co-founder, no advisor, and no domain expertise to suggest
this particular person is the one to build this particular thing.

## Traction & ask

There is no product, no waitlist, no users, no demo, and no revenue — just a
pitch deck and a logo. We have not validated demand with a single prospective
user. We are asking for a $3M seed round to build the chain and the token. We
cannot point to any evidence that anyone wants this, and we have no plan to find
out short of building the whole thing and hoping.
